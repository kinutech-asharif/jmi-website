About This Engine

The B18C Type R engine is one of the most sought-after naturally aspirated 4-cylinder engines ever produced. Direct from Japan, this authentic Type R powerplant delivers exceptional performance and legendary Honda reliability.

This engine has been thoroughly inspected and tested at our facility. Every engine undergoes compression testing, leak testing, and visual inspection before being approved for sale.

Compatible Vehicles

This B18C Type R engine is compatible with the following vehicles:

  • 1997-2001 Acura Integra Type R (DC2) - Direct Fit
  • 1994-2001 Acura Integra GS-R - Compatible with modifications
  • 1992-2000 Honda Civic - Engine swap (requires mounts, axles, ECU)
  • 1993-1997 Honda Del Sol - Engine swap (requires mounts, axles, ECU)
  • 1988-1991 Honda CRX - Engine swap (requires complete swap kit)

Installation Notes

Direct Fit: For 97-01 Integra Type R, this is a direct replacement requiring minimal modification.

Engine Swaps: For other Honda/Acura vehicles, professional installation is recommended. You may need additional parts including motor mounts, axles, shift linkage, exhaust, and wiring adapters.

Our team can provide guidance on swap requirements and recommend trusted installers in your area.

🛡️ 30-Day Warranty Coverage

All engines sold by Japan Motor Import include a 30-day warranty from the date of delivery. This warranty covers internal engine components against defects in materials and workmanship.

What's Covered: Engine block, cylinder head, pistons, crankshaft, camshaft(s), valves, timing components, and oil pump.

✓ Warranty Requirements

To maintain warranty coverage:

  • Installation must be performed by a qualified mechanic
  • Proper oil and coolant must be used
  • Engine must be broken in according to manufacturer recommendations
  • No modifications to internal engine components
  • Retain all documentation and inspection reports

⚠️ What's Not Covered

Warranty does not cover: external accessories (alternator, starter, AC compressor), sensors, wiring harnesses, gaskets, seals, improper installation, abuse, overheating, or modifications.
